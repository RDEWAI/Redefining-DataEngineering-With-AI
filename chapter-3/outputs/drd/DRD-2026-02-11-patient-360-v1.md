## Executive Summary

The Patient 360 Search initiative delivers a unified search interface enabling clinicians and staff to retrieve a complete patient view in under 2 seconds. By consolidating data from the Synthea Healthcare EHR database (5,767 patients, 340K+ encounters, 4.3M+ observations), the system eliminates the current 8-minute multi-system lookup workflow. Success is measured by 90% of searches completing within 2 seconds, zero missed allergy alerts, and 50% reduction in pre-appointment chart review time.

---

## 1. Business Context

### 1.1 Business Request

Clinicians currently navigate three to five separate systems to piece together a complete picture of a patient before each appointment. On average, a physician spends 8 minutes per patient just finding and cross-referencing information across these systems. This fragmented workflow leads to clinician frustration, missed critical information (allergies, recent lab results), inefficient care coordination, and excessive time spent by billing staff on reconciliation.

The Patient 360 Search provides a single search interface where any authorized staff member can type a patient's name and instantly see a consolidated view including demographics, encounters, conditions, medications, lab results, allergies, and billing summary.

### 1.2 Business Objectives

- Reduce clinician time spent on patient lookups from 8 minutes to under 30 seconds
- Eliminate missed allergy alerts by surfacing them prominently in every search result
- Enable care coordinators to review 30+ patients per day (up from 10-15 currently)
- Provide billing staff with instant access to encounter-claims reconciliation
- Support quality reporting by making readmission data easily accessible

### 1.3 Success Criteria

- 90% of searches return complete results within 2 seconds
- Clinician satisfaction score above 4.0 out of 5.0 in post-launch survey
- 50% reduction in pre-appointment chart review time
- Zero missed allergy alerts in the first 90 days
- All active patients (5,767 verified in current system) searchable from day one

## 5. Business Rules

### 5.1 Default Values

| Field | Default Value | When Applied | Business Justification |
|-------|---------------|--------------|------------------------|
| Cost fields (NULL) | 0 | When source value is NULL | Per user confirmation: treat NULL costs as zero for aggregations |
| medication.stop | NULL (active) | When prescription has no end date | Open-ended prescriptions are considered active |
| allergy.severity1 | "Unknown" | When severity not recorded | 56% of allergies lack severity; display as Unknown for safety |

### 5.2 Calculations and Derivations

#### Patient Age

- **Formula / Logic**: `DATEDIFF('year', birthdate, CURRENT_DATE)`
- **Input Fields**: `patients.birthdate`
- **Output Field**: `calculated_age`
- **Business Purpose**: Display patient age in search results and profile
- **Example**: birthdate = 1980-05-15, current date = 2026-02-11 → age = 45

#### Total Visit Cost

- **Formula / Logic**: `COALESCE(encounter.base_encounter_cost, 0) + SUM(COALESCE(procedure.base_cost, 0)) + SUM(COALESCE(medication.totalcost, 0))`
- **Input Fields**: `encounters.base_encounter_cost`, `procedures.base_cost`, `medications.totalcost`
- **Output Field**: `total_visit_cost`
- **Business Purpose**: Billing staff need pre-calculated total cost per encounter
- **Example**: Encounter base $100 + Procedure $250 + Medication $50 = Total $400

#### Readmission Flag (30-Day)

- **Formula / Logic**: `IF inpatient encounter within 30 days of previous inpatient discharge THEN flag = TRUE`
- **Input Fields**: `encounters.encounterclass`, `encounters.start`, `encounters.stop`
- **Output Field**: `is_30_day_readmission`
- **Business Purpose**: Quality reporting requires tracking 30-day readmissions
- **Example**: Discharge 2026-01-15, new inpatient admit 2026-02-01 → readmission = TRUE

#### Active Medication Status

- **Formula / Logic**: `IF medication.stop IS NULL OR medication.stop > CURRENT_DATE THEN status = 'Active' ELSE status = 'Discontinued'`
- **Input Fields**: `medications.stop`
- **Output Field**: `medication_status`
- **Business Purpose**: Physicians need to distinguish active vs discontinued medications
- **Example**: stop = NULL → Active; stop = 2025-12-01 → Discontinued

### 5.3 Transformation Rules

- **Patient Name Display**: Concatenate `prefix + first + middle + last + suffix`, omitting NULL components
- **SSN Masking**: Display only last 4 digits (XXX-XX-1234) for patient disambiguation
- **Date Formatting**: Display all dates in MM/DD/YYYY format for US healthcare context
- **Code Description Priority**: Display human-readable `description` field; show code only on hover/detail view
- **Currency Formatting**: Display costs as USD with two decimals (e.g., $1,234.56)

### 5.4 Edge Cases and Exceptions

- **Scenario**: Patient has multiple insurance payers (primary and secondary)
  - **Expected Behavior**: Display both payers with primary/secondary designation
  - **Rationale**: Per Revenue Cycle Director requirement; common in Medicare patients

- **Scenario**: Patient has common name with multiple matches (e.g., "John Smith")
  - **Expected Behavior**: Display DOB and last 4 of SSN in search results for disambiguation
  - **Rationale**: Per end user physician feedback; prevents wrong-patient errors

- **Scenario**: Deceased patient searched
  - **Expected Behavior**: Display deceased flag prominently with death date
  - **Rationale**: Clinical safety; prevents orders on deceased patients

- **Scenario**: Allergy with NULL severity
  - **Expected Behavior**: Display allergy with severity = "Unknown"; do not suppress the allergy
  - **Rationale**: Patient safety; missing severity does not mean no allergy

- **Scenario**: Search returns 100+ results
  - **Expected Behavior**: Display first 100 sorted by most recent encounter, prompt to refine search
  - **Rationale**: Performance and usability

## 7. Regulatory and Compliance

### 7.1 Applicable Regulations

| Regulation | Scope | Key Requirements | Impact on Data Design |
|------------|-------|------------------|-----------------------|
| HIPAA | All patient health information | Privacy Rule (PHI protection), Security Rule (technical safeguards), Breach Notification | Encryption at rest, access logging, minimum necessary access, 6-year retention |

### 7.2 Data Classification

| Data Element | Classification Level | Handling Requirements |
|-------------|---------------------|----------------------|
| SSN | PHI - High Sensitivity | Mask in UI (show last 4 only), encrypt at rest, audit all access |
| Patient name, DOB, address | PHI - Standard | Encrypt at rest, role-based access, audit logging |
| Medical conditions, medications | PHI - Clinical | Encrypt at rest, clinical role access only, audit logging |
| Allergies | PHI - Clinical (Safety Critical) | Prominent display, encrypt at rest, no suppression allowed |
| Billing/claims data | PHI - Financial | Encrypt at rest, billing role access, audit logging |
| Provider information | Internal | Standard access controls |

### 7.3 Retention Requirements

| Data Category | Retention Period | Deletion Method | Legal Basis |
|--------------|-----------------|-----------------|-------------|
| Adult patient records | 6 years from last encounter | Secure deletion with audit trail | HIPAA minimum |
| Minor patient records | Until age 21 + 6 years | Secure deletion with audit trail | State minor medical record laws |
| Billing/claims records | 7 years | Secure deletion with audit trail | IRS requirements, payer contracts |
| Audit logs | 6 years | Archive to cold storage | HIPAA Security Rule |

### 7.4 Access Controls

| Role / Group | Data Access Level | Restrictions | Authentication |
|-------------|-------------------|--------------|----------------|
| Physicians | Full clinical read | Own patients + emergency override | SSO + MFA (Phase 2) |
| Nurses | Full clinical read | Assigned unit patients | SSO + MFA (Phase 2) |
| Care Coordinators | Panel patient read | Assigned panel only | SSO + MFA (Phase 2) |
| Billing Staff | Demographics + financial | No clinical notes | SSO + MFA (Phase 2) |
| Department Heads | Aggregate reporting | No individual PHI in reports | SSO + MFA (Phase 2) |

### 7.5 Audit Requirements

- **Access Logging**: Log all patient record access with user ID, timestamp, patient ID, and action type
- **Query Logging**: Log all search queries with user ID and search terms
- **Export Logging**: Log any data exports or downloads with user ID and record count
- **Breach Detection**: Alert on anomalous access patterns (volume spikes, off-hours access)
- **Retention**: Maintain audit logs for 6 years per HIPAA Security Rule
