Active-only scoping is critical for conditions and medications -- without it, conditions average 36 per patient and medications 51 per patient, which would create unacceptably large arrays.

## Schema Decisions

### Decision: Expand ARRAY<STRUCT> to Conditions and Medications

**Options Considered**:
1. Keep count-only for conditions/medications, ARRAY<STRUCT> for allergies only
2. Add ARRAY<STRUCT> for all conditions and medications (including resolved)
3. Add ARRAY<STRUCT> for active conditions and active medications only

**Selected**: Option 3

**Rationale**: Active-only scoping bounds cardinality to manageable levels (~10 conditions, ~4 medications per patient) while providing clinically relevant data for the 2-second search SLA. Resolved conditions and stopped medications are historical and not needed for the patient_summary search use case. Eliminates joins to clinical_conditions and clinical_medications at Gold query time.

**Trade-off**: Resolved conditions and stopped medications are not available in the patient_summary array -- consumers needing full history must query patient_clinical_history (which retains count-only grain) or Silver tables directly.
